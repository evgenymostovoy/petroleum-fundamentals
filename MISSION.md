# Mission: Petroleum Engineering, from Fundamentals to Field Decisions

## Why

Become the professional trusted with bigger-picture calls — one who
connects technical, commercial, and strategic dots across field
development choices, project value, and portfolio context. Fifteen years
of D&C practice on the NCS (B.Sc. Gubkin, M.Sc. NTNU) built strong
applied judgment, but patchy, non-automatic theory keeps mental effort on
basics instead of on those bigger calls. The fix is instant recall of two
foundations: petroleum engineering fundamentals across the full
subsurface-to-well span, at strong M.Sc. level, and working quantitative
fluency in upstream economics and development decision-making.

## Success looks like

- Follows and **challenges** geologists, petrophysicists, and reservoir
  engineers *in their own terms*.
- Explains the "why" behind every standard D&C design choice from first
  principles, nothing looked up.
- Traces any D&C design choice to its consequence for cost, schedule,
  recovery, and project value, in a project economist's language.
- Reads a field development plan or investment case and credibly
  critiques its trade-offs (well count, phasing, concept, drainage).
- Colleagues bring cross-discipline, development-planning, and
  investment-framing questions — not only well delivery.

## Constraints

- Not a beginner: rebuild theory underneath daily practice, peer-to-peer
  tone.
- Instant recall is the bar for everything taught → retrieval practice,
  spacing, and interleaving in every lesson.
- Anchor commercial and strategic content to decisions a D&C engineer
  owns or influences — never abstract finance.
- Dual-basin grounding: NCS plus two deepwater Brazil projects (unit and
  example conventions: see NOTES.md).

## Out of scope

- Operational procedures and field practice (already mastered).
- Midstream/downstream engineering depth; corporate finance, accounting,
  and trading at specialist depth.
- Management and leadership soft skills — this workspace builds the
  capability for a bigger role, it is not career coaching.
