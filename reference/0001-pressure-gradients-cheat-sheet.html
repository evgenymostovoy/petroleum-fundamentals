Master formulas

Hydrostatic pressure — SI / NCS p [bar] = 0.0981 · ρ [sg] · TVD [m]
Hydrostatic pressure — field units p [psi] = 0.052 · ρ [ppg] · TVD [ft]
Terzaghi / Biot effective stress σ′ = σ − α·pp    (α ≈ 1 for weak/soft rock)
Equivalent mud weight from a pressure at depth EMW [sg] = p [bar] / (0.0981 · TVD [m])
