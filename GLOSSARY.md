# Petroleum Engineering Glossary

Canonical terminology for this workspace: petroleum engineering
fundamentals, from subsurface through well delivery, plus upstream
economics as the commercial arc develops.

Terms enter this glossary only once the user demonstrably understands
them. The seed below is vocabulary from 15 years of D&C practice
(LR-0001). Theory terms taught in lessons (effective stress, stress
path, undercompaction, hoop stress, Mohr–Coulomb, UCS, …) are promoted
here after correct spaced retrieval — first candidates are pending the
Lesson 0002 quiz round (LR-0002).

## Terms

### Depth & pressure language

**TVD (True Vertical Depth)**:
Vertical distance from the depth datum (RKB unless stated) down to a
point in the well — the depth all pressure calculations use.
_Avoid_: depth (unqualified), vertical depth

**MD (Measured Depth)**:
Distance to a point measured along the wellbore path; always ≥ TVD.
_Avoid_: along-hole depth, driller's depth (as synonyms in writing)

**Pore pressure (p<sub>p</sub>)**:
Pressure of the fluid occupying the rock's pore space.
_Avoid_: formation pressure, reservoir pressure (reserve for the
reservoir's average p<sub>p</sub>)

**Overburden (vertical stress, σ<sub>v</sub>)**:
Total weight per unit area of rock grains, pore fluid, and (offshore)
seawater above a given TVD.
_Avoid_: lithostatic pressure, geostatic pressure

**EMW (Equivalent Mud Weight)**:
A pressure at a TVD re-expressed as the density of a static mud column
that would produce it: EMW [sg] = p [bar] / (0.0981 · TVD [m]).
_Avoid_: equivalent density (ambiguous vs. ECD)

**ECD (Equivalent Circulating Density)**:
The EMW at a depth while circulating — static mud weight plus annular
friction losses expressed as density.
_Avoid_: dynamic density

**Fracture pressure**:
The wellbore pressure at which the formation at a given depth cracks
open and takes whole fluid; its EMW form is the fracture gradient.
_Avoid_: breakdown pressure (reserve for intact-rock initiation)

**Mud weight window**:
The EMW band a section can be drilled in: above pore pressure (and
collapse pressure), below fracture pressure.
_Avoid_: drilling margin (reserve for kick tolerance discussions)

### Well events & tests

**Kick**:
An unplanned influx of formation fluid into the wellbore, occurring when
wellbore pressure falls below pore pressure in a permeable zone.
_Avoid_: influx (unqualified), well flow

**Lost circulation (losses)**:
Whole mud flowing into the formation, when wellbore pressure exceeds
fracture pressure or opens pre-existing fractures.
_Avoid_: seepage (reserve for filtrate-rate losses)

**LOT (Leak-Off Test)**:
Pressurizing the open formation below a new casing shoe until fluid
begins to leak off, measuring the fracture initiation point; anchors the
fracture gradient used for the next section.
_Avoid_: casing test (that tests the casing, not the rock)

**FIT (Formation Integrity Test)**:
Pressurizing below the shoe to a predetermined EMW without inducing
leak-off, verifying the formation holds the planned exposure.
_Avoid_: limit test

**Breakout**:
Paired enlargements on opposite sides of the borehole wall from
compressive shear failure of the rock at the wall.
_Avoid_: washout (fluid erosion — different mechanism), ovalization
(the geometry, not the failure)

**Casing seat (shoe depth)**:
The TVD at which a casing string is set and cemented, chosen so the next
section's mud weight window stays open.
_Avoid_: shoe (alone, when the setting-depth decision is meant)
