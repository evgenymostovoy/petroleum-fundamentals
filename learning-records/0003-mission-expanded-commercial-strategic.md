# Mission expanded: commercial and strategic fluency joins the technical goal

The user articulated that instant-recall fundamentals are the foundation,
not the end goal: the end-state is a professional who connects technical,
commercial, and strategic perspectives. "Strategic" spans field development
decisions, project economics and value, and business/portfolio context,
with career positioning as motivation (explicitly not curriculum).
Economics is to be taught at a working quantitative level — simple
NPV/breakeven reasoning at the same instant-recall bar as the technical
material. MISSION.md rewritten accordingly (the old "converse
intelligently" cap on economics is removed).

## Implications

- Curriculum gains a commercial arc: roughly every fourth or fifth lesson
  is a value/decision lesson that reuses the technical material just
  learned (e.g. after the geomechanics spine: what wellbore-stability
  failures cost and how that risk prices into casing-seat selection).
- Weighting within "strategic": development decisions and project
  economics deepest; portfolio/business level as context lessons only.
- Out of scope remains: specialist finance/accounting/trading depth,
  midstream/downstream engineering depth, leadership soft skills.
