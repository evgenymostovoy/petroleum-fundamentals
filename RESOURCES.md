# Petroleum Engineering Fundamentals Resources

## Knowledge

### Cross-discipline reference

- [SPE PetroWiki](https://petrowiki.spe.org/)
  Peer-reviewed by SPE, derived from the Petroleum Engineering Handbook.
  Free and stable. Use for: first-stop checks and citable URLs in any
  discipline.
- [SLB Energy Glossary](https://glossary.slb.com/)
  The industry's de-facto glossary. Use for: terminology and glossary
  definitions in lessons.

### Geomechanics

- [Book: _Petroleum Related Rock Mechanics_ — Fjær, Holt, Horsrud, Raaen (Elsevier)](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/petroleum-related-rock-mechanics/fjaer/978-0-444-88913-3)
  The NTNU/SINTEF standard text — ideal fit for an NTNU alum. Use for:
  elasticity, effective stress, rock failure, borehole stability.
- Book: _Reservoir Geomechanics_ — Zoback (Cambridge)
  Use for: in-situ stress state, pore pressure, wellbore stability; a free
  companion course exists online (Stanford/edX).

### Drilling & well design

- Book: _Applied Drilling Engineering_ — Bourgoyne, Millheim, Chenevert,
  Young (SPE Textbook Series Vol. 2)
  The classic quantitative drilling text. Use for: pressure calculations,
  hydraulics, casing design derivations.
- Book: _Fundamentals of Drilling Engineering_ — Mitchell & Miska (SPE
  Textbook Series Vol. 12)
  Modern successor with problem sets. Use for: practice problems.
- Book: _Modern Well Design_ — Aadnøy (UiS)
  NCS-flavored well design methodology. Use for: kick tolerance, casing
  seat selection logic.

### Completions

- Book: _Well Completion Design_ — Bellarby (Elsevier, Developments in
  Petroleum Science 56)
  The standard completion design text. Use for: completion architecture,
  tubing stress, sand control, perforating theory.
- Book: _Petroleum Production Systems_ — Economides, Hill,
  Ehlig-Economides, Zhu (2nd ed.)
  Use for: inflow/outflow (nodal) analysis, skin, stimulation, lift.

### Reservoir engineering

- Book: _Fundamentals of Reservoir Engineering_ — Dake (Elsevier)
  The classic first-principles reservoir text. Use for: Darcy flow,
  material balance, well testing from first principles.

### Petrophysics

- Book: _Petrophysics_ — Tiab & Donaldson (Elsevier)
  Use for: porosity, permeability, saturation, log interpretation theory.
- Paper: "The Electrical Resistivity Log as an Aid in Determining Some
  Reservoir Characteristics" — Archie, 1942 (SPE-942054-G, OnePetro)
  The original Archie paper. Use for: first-principles saturation from
  resistivity.

### Geology & geophysics

- Book: _Elements of Petroleum Geology_ — Selley & Sonnenberg
  Use for: source–reservoir–seal–trap systems, basin context.
- Book: _Petroleum Geoscience_ — Gluyas & Swarbrick
  Use for: the geology↔engineering bridge, incl. overpressure mechanisms
  (Swarbrick is a pore-pressure authority).

### Fluids & thermodynamics

- Book: _The Properties of Petroleum Fluids_ — McCain
  Use for: PVT and phase behavior fundamentals.
- Book: _Phase Behavior_ — Whitson & Brulé (SPE Monograph 20)
  NTNU-linked (Whitson). Use for: deeper PVT and EOS work.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [SPE — Society of Petroleum Engineers](https://www.spe.org/)
  Local NCS sections (Stavanger, Bergen, Oslo) run technical meetings.
  Use for: testing cross-discipline reasoning against practitioners;
  OnePetro paper access likely via membership/employer.
- Workplace subsurface team (geologists, petrophysicists, reservoir
  engineers)
  The mission's real proving ground. Use for: deliberately asking "why"
  questions after each lesson and stress-testing recall in meetings.
